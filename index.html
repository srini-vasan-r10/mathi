Dr. Kaliyan Mathiyazhagan serves as Full Professor & Head of the Research Centre at Thiagarajar School of Management (TSM), Madurai, and continuing Visiting Faculty at Tor Vergata University of Rome, Italy. With over 16 years of academic leadership, his work equips global industries and policymakers to transition from linear models to sustainable, data-driven systems.
